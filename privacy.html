Privacy Policy

Data stored by the local server

The server stores account identifiers, password hashes, wallet metadata, encrypted wallet blobs, sessions, notifications and audit events in server.json.

Passwords

Passwords are processed with scrypt and only password hashes are stored. Plaintext passwords are not written to server.json.

Wallet secrets

The included client encrypts wallet material before sending it to the server. The server stores ciphertext and does not expose a wallet-decryption API.

Local deployment

Keep server.json and your master key private. Do not commit them to source control. Use HTTPS and secure cookies when deploying outside localhost.
